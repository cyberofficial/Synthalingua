📊 Compression Ratio

1.9 2.4 3.0
What it does: Prevents the model from getting stuck and repeating phrases by checking text compressibility.
Default (2.4): Whisper's recommended value, works well for most content.
Lower (1.9-2.3): Stricter filtering - use when model produces repetitive text or with non-speech audio.
Higher (2.5-3.0): More lenient - use with complex/technical speech that naturally compresses less.
⚠️ Note:
💡 Pro Tips:
  • Music/Non-speech: Lower to 1.9-2.0 to prevent hallucinations like "I'm not going to lie..." repeating.
  • Larger models (medium/large): May need lower threshold (2.0-2.2) as they're more "wordy" under uncertainty.
  • Smaller models (base/tiny): Default 2.4 works well. Lower threshold may reject valid speech.
  • Technical/Complex speech: Raise to 2.6-3.0 if valid transcriptions are being rejected.
