Before you start

            
Synthalingua runs speech recognition and optional translation locally on this machine. It does not upload your audio or video anywhere on its own.

            
Transcription downloads nothing in packaged builds: the models ship with the app and are read from the local models folder. A stream URL or a Discord webhook sends data to that service only because you configured it.

            
Use this software only on media you have the right to process. The authors are not responsible for how it is used.
